## 0. Methodological Framing: Why Strip the Horse and the Choreography?

The horse and the dance steps are the **surface** of the comparison — the easiest things for diffusion (or coincidence) to explain. The deep structure lives in four domains that could exist in total isolation from both:

## 2. Domain A — Oath-Bands & Sworn Male Fraternities

### Core sources

1. 🔗 **Jathilan Horse Dance: Spirit Possession Beliefs and Practices in Present-Day Java** (2020) — [journal.ugm.ac.id/ikat/article/view/37389](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/ikat/article/view/37389). *Documents troupe-level organization: the pawang's ritual authority, the disciplined band structure, and possession as a collective troupe property — the Malay-world near-equivalent of the sworn band, minus the formal oath.*
2. 🔗 **Persediaan untuk Trance Dance: kajian kes Penari Kuda Kepang di Kluang, Johor** (2015) — [ir.upsi.edu.my/detailsg.php?det=9125](https://ir.upsi.edu.my/detailsg.php?det=9125). *Documents the ascetic preparation regime — fasting, abstinence, ritual discipline (tirakat) — before performance.*
3. 📚 **Gail Kligman, *Căluș: Symbolic Transformation in Romanian Ritual* (University of Chicago Press, 1981).** *The definitive monograph: the swearing ceremony on the flag (steag), sword, garlic and wormwood bundle (peliniță); the cursing-oath; 9–14 days of sexual abstinence; the vătaf (leader)/mut (fool) hierarchy.*
4. 📚 **Kim McCone, "Hounds, Warbands and the *Koryos*"** (Indogermanische Forschungen 92, 1987). *Reconstructs PIE \*kóryos — the youthful male war-band living outside the village, under oath, ritually liminal.*
5. 📚 **David W. Anthony, *The Horse, the Wheel, and Language* (Princeton, 2007).** *Models the PIE \*koryos youth band and its spread with steppe expansions.*
6. 📚 **Stig Wikander, "Der arische Männerbund" (Lund, 1938); Otto Höfler, *Kultische Geheimbünde der Germanen* (1934).** *The founding texts of IE Männerbund studies — dated and criticized, but they define the category.*
7. 📚 **Hutton Webster, *Primitive Secret Societies* (1908, rev. 1932).** *The classic cross-cultural baseline: age-grade sodalities with initiation ordeal, secrecy oath and periodic license — oath-bands are a widespread independent solution, not an IE monopoly.*
8. 📚 **Paul Spencer on East African age-sets; Victor Turner, *The Ritual Process* (1969) on liminality and communitas.** *Turner's liminal-communitas framework fits both the Căluș band and the Jathilan troupe — a Hypothesis 1 engine.*

### Structural findings for Domain A
- **Match (weak-to-moderate)**: liminality, male exclusivity, leader/fool hierarchy (vătaf/mut ≈ pawang/dancer roles), pre-performance abstinence.
- **Honest asymmetry**: the Călușari's *formal, cursing, death-sanctioned oath on sacred objects* has **no documented equal in Kuda Kepang**. This asymmetry is diagnostic — the oath-band form is a Balkan/IE-specific crystallization, while the shared part is the thinner universal (liminal male collective + ritual discipline).

> ✅ **Consensus read**: PIE \*kóryos — mainstream post-Anthony (2007), contested in details. Höfler — largely superseded. Webster/Turner — canonical. Kligman — the unchallenged standard on the Căluș oath.

## 3. Domain B — Trance-Healing as a Cult of Affliction

### Core sources
1. 📚 **Ioan M. Lewis, *Ecstatic Religion* (Penguin, 1971; 2nd ed. Routledge, 1989).** *The keystone. Peripheral possession (spirits cause misfortune/illness in the marginalized) vs central possession (controlled, performed possession by ritual adepts). Călușari: iele afflict → performance cures. Jathilan: troupe performs possession → cures clients.*
2. 🔗 **Kuda Kepang: A Case Report of Javanese Cultural-Related Trance** (2019) — [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6876829/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6876829/). *The endang-calling, the volitional structure of possession, and its ICD-10 F44.3 classification.*
3. 🔗 **Jathilan Horse Dance** (2020) — [journal.ugm.ac.id/ikat/article/view/37389](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/ikat/article/view/37389). *Dancers are possessed by tiger, monkey and buto (ogre) spirits as well as horse spirits — the possession complex precedes and exceeds the horse.*
4. 📚 **Erika Bourguignon, "Spirit Possession and Altered States of Consciousness" (1973/1980).** *HRAF-based finding: ~90% of sampled societies institutionalize at least one altered-state practice.*
5. 📚 **Janice Boddy, *Wombs and Alien Spirits* (1989) — Sudanese zar; Michael Lambek, *Human Spirits* (1981) — Mayotte tromba.** *The standard "clean control" ethnographies of cults of affliction — no horses, same architecture.*
6. 📚 **Main Puteri (Terengganu, Malaysia).** *Malay healing séance: puteri spirits (female, seductive, illness-causing) induced to possess healer and patient for diagnosis and cure — the closest structural rhyme to the iele-cure in the Malay world.*
7. 🔗 **Nestinarstvo (Bulgarian fire-dancing trance healing)** — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestinarstvo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestinarstvo). *Balkan control case: trance-healing with no horse — the Balkans generate the grammar independently of the căluș form.*
8. 📚 **South Slavic parallels**: Bulgarian Kalushari/Rusalii and Serbian rusalje (women's trance-healing brotherhoods afflicted by ruse vile). *The affliction-cure complex is a Balkan-wide family, not a Romanian singularity.*

### Structural findings for Domain B
- **Match (strong)**: both sit squarely in Lewis's cult-of-affliction architecture — a named class of illness-causing spirits (iele ↔ endang/puteri/jin), specialists who traffic with them, cure-through-performance, calendrical vulnerability windows.
- **This is the strongest domain.** Lewis's model was built on Africa/Middle East data — and both traditions slot in cleanly. That is what a universal-grammar hit looks like.

> ✅ **Consensus read**: cult-of-affliction model — high consensus. Bourguignon's 90% — robust. Lewis's "peripheral vs central" — standard.

## 4. Domain C — Effigy Logic (The Object as Spirit-Vehicle)

### Core sources
1. 🔗 **Kuda Lumping — Wikipedia** — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuda_Lumping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuda_Lumping). *The flat woven horse as the standard prop — a woven "cage", not a sculpture; the flatness encodes ridability by spirit and dancer alike.*
2. 🔗 **SEA Junction — Kuda-Kuda Lumping (Flat Horses)** — [seajunction.org/gallery/glimpses-of-southeast-asia/kuda-kuda-lumping-flat-horses-performance](https://seajunction.org/gallery/glimpses-of-southeast-asia/kuda-kuda-lumping-flat-horses-performance). *"Dancers act out riding flat effigies... possessed by the spirit of the horses" — the animacy claim directly.*
3. 📚 **E.C. Cawte, *Ritual Animal Disguise* (1978).** *The standard survey of British/European animal-effigy ritual (Mari Lwyd, Old Horse, Padstow 'Obby 'Oss, Hooden Horse) — a pan-European effigy-animal grammar, independent of any trance complex.*
4. 📚 **Violet Alford, *The Hobby Horse and Other Animal Masks* (1978).** *Extends to continental forms — the European horse-effigy is widespread, varied, and mostly trance-free.*
5. 📚 **Romanian effigy grammar beyond the horse**: the steag (flag as portable shrine — Kligman); the peliniță wormwood bundle; New Year masked effigies (Capra, Ursul, Brezaia).
6. 📚 **Balinese/Indonesian effigy complex**: barongan (tiger-lion effigy ridden by possessed dancers), barong–Rangda, barong landung. *The flat horse is one member of a family; effigy logic in Java does not need the horse to exist.*
7. 📚 **Tamil *Poykal Kudhirai* ("false-leg horse") dance** — a possible Indic bridge for effigy-form, flagged as secondary.

### Structural findings for Domain C
- **Match (moderate)**: both treat portable objects (flat horse / flag+peliniță) as enlivened power-addresses — but the specific flat-horse prop has dense independent European and archipelago parallels, so the shared prop is convergent craft logic, not a borrowed artifact.
- Key negative control: most European hobby horses are **not** possessed; most Javanese effigies are. The pairing *effigy + possession* is the real signal.

> ✅ **Consensus read**: Cawte/Alford — standard references. Effigy-as-vehicle in Indonesian ritual — well established. No one claims direct transmission on prop evidence alone.

## 5. Domain D — Dysphoric Ritual (Pain-Proofing as Proof of Possession)

### Core sources
1. 🔗 **Kuda Lumping — Wikipedia (glass-eating, whipping immunity)** — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuda_Lumping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuda_Lumping). *The full dysphoric package: voluntary ordeal + pain-proofing as public evidence of the spirit's presence.*
2. 🔗 **Kuda Kepang case report (PMC)** — [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6876829/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6876829/). *Confirms the ordeal repertoire is confined to the trance state — ordeal as an on/off verifier of possession.*
3. 🔗 **Dimitris Xygalatas, *The Burning Saints* (Acumen, 2012)** — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastenaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastenaria). *The Greek Anastenaria: Orthodox-calendar trance fire-walking with healing claims, no horse — the same seasonal-saint calendar logic as Călușari.*
4. 🔗 **Konvalinka et al. (2011), "Synchronized arousal... in a fire-walking ritual," *PNAS* 108(20)** — [pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1016954108](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1016954108). *Dysphoric ritual synchronizes arousal in performers and spectators — Hypothesis 1's engine, measured.*
5. 🔗 **Xygalatas et al. (2013), "Extreme rituals promote prosociality," *Psychological Science*** — [journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797612472910](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797612472910). *Kavadi bearers (piercing ordeal) show increased charity — costly signaling produces real social goods; applies to Thaipusam.*
6. 📚 **Harvey Whitehouse, *Arguments and Icons* (Oxford, 2000); "modes of religiosity".** *The imagistic mode explains oath-band (A) and ordeal (D) as one cognitive package — the strongest theoretical glue in the dossier.*
7. 📚 **Malay-world clean comparanda**: Thaipusam kavadi piercing; Chinese tang-ki spirit mediums; Balinese ngurek self-stabbing and mesabatan api. *Three independent dysphoric institutions flourish inside the Malay world without any horse.*

### Structural findings for Domain D
- **Match (strong)**: pain-proofing as public verification of possession is identical in kind across both — and absent from most European horse-effigy traditions. The dysphoric element traveled with the possession complex, not the horse.

> ✅ **Consensus read**: costly-signaling and arousal-synchrony (Konvalinka 2011, Xygalatas 2013) — replicated. Whitehouse's imagistic mode — mainstream. Glass-eating in Java — documented, uncontroversial.

## 6. Hypothesis Stack 1 — Convergent Ritual Logic (Universalist)

**Sources**: Lewis · Bourguignon · Turner · Rappaport, *Ritual and Religion in the Making of Humanity* (1999) · Lawson & McCauley, *Rethinking Religion* (1990) · Whitehouse · Sosis & Bulbulia (costly signaling) · Winkelman (neuroanthropology of shamanism) · empirical anchors (Konvalinka 2011; Xygalatas 2013).

**What it explains**: why four domains co-occur in both traditions; why the Malay world generates the grammar horse-free (barongan, tang-ki, Thaipusam, main puteri); why the Balkans generate it horse-free (Anastenaria, Nestinarstvo, rusalje); Bourguignon's 90% floor; the physiological binding mechanism.

**What it cannot explain**: the shared *symbolic content* — the horse specifically, the warrior-rider prestige, the kuda/aśva lexical layer. For that, Hypothesis 2.

## 7. Hypothesis Stack 2 — Common Indo-European / Steppe Source

**Sources**: McCone 1987 · Anthony 2007 · Mallory & Adams, *The Oxford Introduction to Proto-Indo-European* · Kristiansen & Larsson, *The Rise of Bronze Age Society* (2005) · Dumézil, *The Destiny of the Warrior* / *Mitra-Varuna* · Bruce Lincoln, *Myth, Cosmos, and Society* (1986) · Wikander 1938 / Höfler 1934.

**The bridge into the archipelago**: the Sanskrit aśva package — the Aśvavarman inscription (Kutai, c. 400 CE); loanword kuda < Indic; Thaipusam and tang-ki demonstrating that Indic and Chinese ordeal-grammar demonstrably flows into the archipelago — so an Indic-carried layer is mechanistically demonstrated, not just imagined.

**Critical controls**: Kligman's etymological caution (Romanian căluș may derive from "club/wooden bar", the horse-reading contested); Höfler's cultic-band thesis largely rejected; Lincoln's warning against pan-IE overreach; no retrieved source connects Călușari to Java, and none connects Rome to the archipelago.

## 9. Updated Synthesis

1. **Domain B (affliction-cure) and Domain D (dysphoric ordeal) now carry the comparison alone.** Both traditions match on them and each region generates them horse-free — the strongest possible configuration for convergent/universal ritual logic (Hypothesis 1).
2. **Domain A (oath-band) splits**: the liminal male collective is universal (Webster/Turner); the cursing-oath on sacred objects is Balkan/IE-specific. The oath need not have travelled to Java for the rest of the grammar to match.
3. **Domain C (effigy logic) confirms independent convergence**: European horse-effigies are overwhelmingly trance-free; archipelago effigies are overwhelmingly possession-laden; the combination is what both traditions share — generated by Domain B + D logic, not by borrowing a prop.
4. **Hypothesis 2 survives in reduced, precise form**: it explains the symbolic layer (horse-prestige, rider-warrior ideology, the kuda/aśva lexicon) that reached Java via Indicization — a mechanism demonstrated by the ordeal-grammar traffic (Thaipusam, tang-ki) flowing the same sea roads. It does not need to explain the ritual grammar, which converges on its own.
5. **Portuguese and Roman relay hypotheses are now effectively eliminated** for the grammar — they can at most have decorated the symbol layer.

### Discriminating tests for the deep-dive
- **Test 1**: Do iele-affliction and puteri-affliction narratives share the same symptom catalogue (sleeplessness, screaming, sexuotropic behaviour)? If yes, this is grammar, not diffusion.
- **Test 2**: Compare oath formulas directly (Kligman's swearing text vs any Malay troupe sumpah). Prediction: no shared formula — confirming Domain A asymmetry.
- **Test 3**: Possession-spirit taxonomy mapping (tiger/monkey/buto vs iele/rusalii) — if the non-horse spirit classes structurally rhyme, the possession complex is the shared unit.
- **Test 4**: Seasonal windows (Rusalii ↔ post-harvest) — test whether both mark the same agrarian hinge functionally, independent of calendar religion.
